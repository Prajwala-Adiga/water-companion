# Character artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this desktop companion. Included asset: `assets/fahadh.png`. The program slices the sheet into four animation frames at runtime. This is an unofficial fan-made cartoon companion, with no affiliation or endorsement implied.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent desktop companion animation sprite sheet. Create a charming recognizable cartoon of Malayalam movie actor Fahadh Faasil, expressive large eyes, short curly dark hair, beard and moustache, warm brown skin, teal casual shirt, cream trousers, brown shoes. Full body chibi proportions, polished hand-drawn digital illustration. Exactly FOUR equal-width cells in ONE horizontal row, each cell contains the SAME character at identical scale and feet baseline with generous transparent margins. Cell 1 walking right with left foot forward; cell 2 walking right with feet passing; cell 3 walking right with right foot forward; cell 4 standing relaxed front three-quarter view with one hand raised greeting. All four full bodies entirely within their own cell. Transparent background, no ground, no shadows outside character, no text, no labels, no borders, no props. Wide landscape sprite sheet suitable for four equal slices.

## Expression frames

The reminder, happy, and snooze frames were generated with the built-in image-generation tool using `assets/fahadh.png` as the character and style reference. A user-supplied still from *Kumbalangi Nights* was used only as the pose and playful-expression reference for the reminder frame.

- `assets/fahadh-reminder.png`: playful tilted-head, hands-on-hips reminder pose.
- `assets/fahadh-happy.png`: warm smile and thumbs-up after Done.
- `assets/fahadh-snooze.png`: gently disappointed, humorous pout after Snooze.

All three prompts requested a single full-body character matching the existing teal shirt, cream trousers, brown shoes, facial hair, hairstyle, and 3D caricature style on a transparent background, with no text, scenery, props, watermark, cropped limbs, or additional people.

## Ranga character

The optional Ranga profile was generated with the built-in image-generation tool from three user-supplied *Aavesham* references. The references anchored the sunglasses and moustache, white shirt and heavy gold jewelry, and full-body swagger respectively.

- `assets/ranga-walk.png`: four equal-width walking cells without a weapon.
- `assets/ranga-reminder.png`: raised-finger hydration warning with a small stylized knife held safely downward.
- `assets/ranga-warning.png`: closed/lowered knife and wagging-finger Snooze warning.
- `assets/ranga-happy.png`: friendly approval while putting the knife back into his pocket.

The prompts requested a polished transparent 3D caricature with a white shirt, dark trousers, brown shoes, layered gold chains, rings, bracelets, aviator sunglasses, thick moustache, and theatrical swagger. They explicitly excluded injury, blood, attacks, aimed weapons, victims, scenery, text, watermarks, and cropped limbs.
